## Premise

- China runs the global cashmere supply chain. ~70–80% of raw cashmere comes from the Mongolia region; nearly all sorting, grading, auctions, and primary processing (scouring, dehairing, combing, spinning, dyeing) is controlled or handled by Chinese cashmere companies.
- The processing steps — not European weaving — determine softness, micron grade, colourfastness, and pilling resistance.
- Western luxury brands import Chinese yarn, knit it in Italy/Scotland, and mark it up 10x. Buying direct from Chinese mills/boutique workshops gets ~90% of the same quality for a fraction of the cost.
- Exception: the "baby cashmere" super-soft tier (e.g. Loro Piana) is genuinely different; it is sourceable but expect ~$200–300+ per sweater.

## How to Search (core workflow)

1. Search Taobao using the **Chinese terms locals use** (see Search Terms below).
2. On each listing, screenshot and translate the description to confirm technical specs: **ply, GSM/weight, yarn source, blend %**.
3. Chinese sellers usually list all technical details — read them; don't trust photos alone.

## Quality Tiers

### Tier 1 — Base / Everyday (Lightweight)
- **Ply:** 1-ply or light 2-ply
- **GSM:** ~180–220
- **Feel:** Light, soft, breathable; indoor wear / layering
- **Western equivalent:** Uniqlo £109 / John Lewis £99 / M&S £99
- **Target price (Taobao/Consinee):** £45–£55
- **Reference listing:** https://m.intl.taobao.com/detail/detail.html?id=764449442159

### Tier 2 — Mid-Weight (Warmer / Thicker)
- **Ply:** 2-ply (proper, dense)
- **GSM:** ~260–320
- **Feel:** Noticeably warmer, holds structure; winter without bulk
- **Western equivalent:** £250–£350 designer cashmere
- **Target price:** £90–£120

### Tier 3 — Heavyweight (Cold Weather / Premium)
- **Ply:** 3-ply or 4-ply
- **GSM:** ~350–450
- **Feel:** Thick, luxurious, proper winter weight; Loro Piana / Zegna feel
- **Western equivalent:** £600–£900+
- **Target price:** £150–£220

### Tier 0 — Baby Cashmere (super-soft, Loro-grade)
- Genuinely softer top tier; sourceable but premium.
- **Target price:** ~$200–300+ per sweater.

## Other Options / Notes

- **La Califfa** — good quality, pricier than the Taobao shops here; a direct dupe option for high-end designs (e.g. a ~$250 "Loro" baby cashmere jumper — noted as strong value).
- **Domestic alternative (UK):** Vinted / eBay for brand-new-with-tags cashmere (e.g. John Lewis, made in Italy, £40–£50 secondhand vs £99 retail) — comparable to entry-tier China sourcing.
